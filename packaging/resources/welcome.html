Ghost

Browse the web together with Mia and your friends. Ask about anything on a page, and let Mia do tasks for you.

This installs Ghost for Google Chrome. It takes about a minute, and you don't need to install anything else first.

Mia uses your own Anthropic account through Claude Code. If you already have Claude Code, Mia uses it; if not, the installer installs it with Anthropic's official installer.
