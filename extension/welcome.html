Mia is in Chrome

Your browser assistant: she reads, compares and acts on your tabs with you.

One more step on this Mac

Mia runs on your computer, not in the cloud. Chrome extensions can't install programs, so there is one download: the Mia installer. Open it, click Install, and Mia connects by herself.

Download the Mia installer
  1. Open the download (bottom of this window, or your Downloads folder)
  2. Click Install and enter your Mac password when asked
  3. Come back to this tab. It turns green when Mia is ready

Waiting for the installer…

This page checks every few seconds. Nothing to click.

What the installer adds, and how to remove it

It puts the Mia helper in /Library/Application Support/Ghost: its own copy of Python and Mia's programs, plus the small program Chrome uses to start them. If you don't have Claude Code, it installs it with Anthropic's official installer; Mia answers with your own Claude account, under Anthropic's terms. Nothing runs until Chrome asks for it. The uninstall.sh file in that folder removes everything.
