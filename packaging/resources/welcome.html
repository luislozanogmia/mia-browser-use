Mia

Mia is your browser assistant in Chrome: she and her bots read, compare and act on your tabs with you, and she asks before anything that sends, posts, buys or deletes.

This installs the Mia helper, which runs on this Mac (Mia doesn't run in the cloud). It takes about a minute and nothing else needs to be installed first. If Chrome doesn't have the Mia extension yet, Chrome offers it the next time it starts.

Mia answers with your own Anthropic account through Claude Code. If you already have Claude Code, Mia uses it; if not, the installer installs it with Anthropic's official installer. Claude Code and Claude are provided by Anthropic under Anthropic's terms; Mia does not guarantee Anthropic's service or model output.

Everything goes in /Library/Application Support/Ghost. The uninstall.sh file in that folder removes it all.
